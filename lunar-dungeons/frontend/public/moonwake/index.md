# The Moonwake Testament — Complete Story

The complete canonical manuscript is arranged into seven volumes.
Together, the six narrative volumes and the world prologue contain approximately 4,972 lines.

## Reading order

1. [The World Before the Journey](00-the-world-before-the-journey.md) — the Lumen, the five notes, the Quiet, the Keepers, and the Wayfarer's arrival.
2. [Act I: The Bell Beneath the Roots](01-act-one-the-bell-beneath-the-roots.md) — Mosslight Wilds, Iri, Pell, the Verdant Colossus, and Return.
3. [Act II: The Hearth That Kept the Dark](02-act-two-the-hearth-that-kept-the-dark.md) — Ember Hollow, Vey, the Ember Sovereign, and Hearth.
4. [Act III: The Library of Unsaid Names](03-act-three-the-library-of-unsaid-names.md) — Frostglass Vale, Edda, the Hollow Monarch, and Name.
5. [Act IV: An Atlas of Impossible Stars](04-act-four-an-atlas-of-impossible-stars.md) — the Astral Archive, Nym, the Astral Warden, and Distance.
6. [Act V: The Quiet Between Heartbeats](05-act-five-the-quiet-between-heartbeats.md) — Hushwater, Auralis, Orin, Witness, the Underlight, and the three endings.
7. [Companions, Creatures, and Afterstories](06-companions-creatures-and-afterstories.md) — character histories, supporting characters, bestiary folios, companion threads, side stories, and epilogues.

## The five notes

**Return** leaves a way home. **Hearth** shares warmth without debt. **Name** belongs to the person who carries it. **Distance** gives separate lives room to meet. **Witness** sees another person without taking their story.

The sixth space is the **Margin**: the freedom to begin a page that the old story could not predict.

## Canon and choices

The Wayfarer is intentionally unnamed. They are not a secret heir or a chosen monarch.
The campaign's three endings are all valid futures: **The Shared Light**, **The Quiet Keeper**, and **The Open Sky**.
The Underlight is an optional secret chapter that reveals the Margin and the purpose of the Unwritten Warden.
